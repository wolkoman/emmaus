## Stream key and auto-start

Both use the channel's default stream key. YouTube ties that key to one
broadcast with auto-start at a time, so auto-start is on for the test only.
After the test on 15 November has ended, open the real stream in Studio and turn
"Automatisch starten" on (and confirm), otherwise someone must press
"Livestream starten" there on the 21st. Auto-stop stays off on both, so a short
network drop does not end the broadcast.
